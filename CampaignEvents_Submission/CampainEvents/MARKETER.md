# Marketer Scenario: Delivered Increased, Opened Decreased

Higher delivery with lower opens does not automatically mean campaign performance worsened.

## Possible causes
- More contacts received the campaign, but a smaller proportion opened it.
- Opens may arrive later than delivery events.
- Email privacy features or blocked images affect open tracking.
- Tracking pixels or provider integrations changed.
- Duplicate, missing or delayed events affect reporting.
- Audience, subject line or send time changed.

## Investigation
1. Confirm the campaign and reporting time window.
2. Compare unique delivered and unique opened contacts.
3. Calculate open rate using a consistent denominator.
4. Segment by provider, domain, audience and send time.
5. Check ingestion delays, duplicates and invalid events.
6. Verify tracking pixels and provider reports.
7. Compare after allowing a consistent observation window.

## Response to Marketer
"Delivered volume increased while recorded opens decreased. We are checking whether this is a genuine engagement change or a tracking/reporting issue. We will compare unique-contact metrics, inspect provider-level data and event delays, and verify open tracking before recommending campaign changes."
